Delete or keep.

Delete Me helps you find the junk photos hiding in your library. Swipe left to delete, right to keep, and say goodbye to full storage. No account, no cloud, no AI. Everything happens on your phone, even on a plane.

Why Delete Me

Storage full. Again.

Thousands of photos and no idea where to start. Delete Me breaks your library into parts of 30, so you can clear a month in a few minutes.

What it does

Small parts, clear choices.

By month

Your library is grouped by month and split into parts of up to 30 photos. Each part shows how far you've got.

Today

On this day from earlier years, your newest photos, and a random 30 that changes each day.

Tap or swipe

Keep or Delete with a tap, or swipe right to keep and left to delete. Undo takes back the last one.

Nothing goes until you confirm

At the end of a part you see every photo you marked. Tap one to keep it, then delete the rest in one go.

Pick up where you left off

Every choice is saved as you make it. Close the app and carry on later from the same photo.

Private

Delete Me has no account and no server. Your photos are never uploaded, and nothing leaves your phone.
