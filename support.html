Support

Why does Delete Me need full access?

To show your whole library, month by month. Everything stays on your phone: Delete Me has no server, so your photos are never uploaded or seen by anyone. With Limited Access, it only sees the photos you picked. To change it, open Settings, tap Apps, then Delete Me, then Photos, and choose Full Access.

Where do deleted photos go?

To Recently Deleted in the Photos app, like any photo you delete. You can recover them there for 30 days.

Why does my iPhone ask again before deleting?

iOS always asks before an app deletes photos. Delete Me can't skip that step, and nothing is deleted until you allow it.

I left in the middle of a part.

Open it again from Home. It picks up at the first photo you haven't decided on, with your earlier choices saved.

How do I start over?

Tap the gear on Home, then Reset progress. Every photo you haven't deleted comes back for review.

Does Delete Me collect my data?

No. There is no account and no server. Read the privacy policy.
